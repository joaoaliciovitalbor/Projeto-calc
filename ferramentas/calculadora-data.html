Date Calculator

Find a date by adding or subtracting calendar days from a starting date.

Resulting date

How it works

Calendar-day addition and subtraction move forward or backward by the exact number of days you enter. Weekends and holidays are included because this tool uses calendar days, not business days.

Practical example

For example, adding 10 days to March 1 returns March 11. Subtracting 10 days from March 1 returns February 20 in a non-leap year.
