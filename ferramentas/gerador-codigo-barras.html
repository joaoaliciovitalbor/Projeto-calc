Barcode Generator

Create an EAN-13 or Code128 barcode and download it as a PNG image.

EAN-13 accepts 12 digits (the check digit is calculated) or 13 digits with a check digit.

Generated barcode

How it works

EAN-13 identifies products with 13 digits. Enter the first 12 digits to calculate the check digit, or enter all 13 digits for validation. Code128 can encode text and numbers in a more flexible sequence. The barcode is generated in your browser using JsBarcode; the download button saves the displayed image as a PNG.

Practical example

Select EAN-13 and enter 590123412345 to calculate the check digit and draw the barcode. For internal identifiers with letters and numbers, select Code128 and enter the desired content. Always validate the code and printout with the scanning system used in your operation.
