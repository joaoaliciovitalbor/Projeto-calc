Excel Formula Builder

Create a formula from common spreadsheet tasks and your cell references.

How it works

Choose a formula task, enter cell references, and build the result. Formula names and argument separators follow the selected site language. The builder does not access or modify your workbook.

Practical example

To total cells A1 through A10, choose “Sum a range” and use A1:A10. Copy the generated formula into the destination cell in Excel or another compatible spreadsheet application.
