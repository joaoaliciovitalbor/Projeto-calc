Ratio and Mixture Calculator 1:N

Calculate the concentrate and diluent volumes for a mixture and see an estimated cost per liter.

The ratio is 1 part concentrate to N parts diluent.

Mixture quantities

How it works

In a 1:N ratio, one part is concentrate and N parts are diluent. There are N + 1 total parts. The calculator divides the final volume by that total to find each part and multiplies the concentrate cost per liter by the concentrate fraction to estimate the mixture's unit cost, assuming the diluent has no cost.

Practical example

To prepare 1 liter at a 1:4 ratio, use 0.2 liters (200 mL) of concentrate and 0.8 liters (800 mL) of diluent. If the concentrate costs R$ 50.00 per liter and the diluent is assumed to be free, the estimated final mixture cost is R$ 10.00 per liter.
