How it works

The counter updates locally and reports characters, non-whitespace characters, words, lines, paragraphs, and UTF-8 byte length. Unicode code points are counted as characters.

Practical example

Paste a short announcement to check its word count and line breaks before publishing it. Use the character total to review form limits or message-length requirements.
