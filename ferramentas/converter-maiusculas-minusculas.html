Uppercase and Lowercase Converter

Change the letter case of text without changing its line breaks.

How it works

Choose uppercase or lowercase to transform the text in your browser. Line breaks and punctuation are preserved, and locale-aware conversion supports accented characters.

Practical example

Paste a list of names or headings, convert them to the capitalization required by a form, and copy the result into your document.
