Price estimate

How it works

The target markup is applied to cost, then the price is adjusted for fees charged on the sale. Net profit subtracts both the original cost and the estimated variable fees. Net margin is net profit divided by the selling price.

Practical example

With a cost of R$ 100, a 50% markup, and no variable fees, the estimated selling price is R$ 150 and the net profit is R$ 50. Add card or marketplace fees to estimate the price needed to preserve that profit.
