Mixture and Dilution Calculator

Calculate component volumes and estimate the cost per liter of a 1:N mixture.

The ratio is 1 part concentrate to N parts diluent.

Mixture quantities

How it works

In a 1:N ratio, one part is concentrate and N parts are diluent. The total volume is divided by N + 1 to find each part. Estimated cost assumes that only the concentrate has a cost.

Practical example

For 1 liter at 1:4, use 0.2 liters of concentrate and 0.8 liters of diluent. If concentrate costs R$ 50 per liter and diluent is treated as free, estimated mixture cost is R$ 10 per liter.
