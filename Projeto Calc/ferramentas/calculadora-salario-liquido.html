Monthly estimate

How it works

The estimate applies progressive employee INSS contributions up to the 2026 contribution ceiling. For IRRF, it compares INSS plus the dependent deduction with the monthly simplified deduction, uses the larger deduction, then applies the 2026 monthly tax table and eligible tax reduction. Other entered deductions are subtracted after the tax estimates.

Reference tables: Receita Federal 2026 income-tax tables and the 2026 INSS contribution table. Confirm the current values with payroll before relying on an estimate.

Practical example

Enter the gross monthly salary and number of tax dependents shown in your records. Compare the estimate with a recent payslip; benefits, additional pay, pension orders, and employer-specific deductions can change the final amount.
