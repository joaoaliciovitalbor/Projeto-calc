Gross estimate

    How it works

    The calculator estimates salary balance, proportional 13th salary and vacation pay, accrued vacation with the one-third addition, eligible indemnified notice, and the FGTS termination fine for the selected scenario. You enter the eligible months and accrued balances because those depend on the employment record.

    Practical example

    Enter the salary and termination details from the employment record, including the FGTS balance. Review each line separately and compare the estimate with the employer's termination statement or a qualified payroll professional before relying on it.
