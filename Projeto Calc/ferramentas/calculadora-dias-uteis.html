Business Days Calculator

Count calendar and business days between dates to organize deadlines and deliveries.

Business days include Monday through Friday and do not exclude holidays.

Date range result

How it works

The calendar-day difference excludes the start date. Business days use the same interval, from the day after the start date through and including the end date, and count only Monday through Friday. National, state, local, and special-calendar holidays are not excluded.

Practical example

From Monday to Friday of the same week, there are 4 calendar days of difference and 4 business days counted after the start date, including Friday. For deadlines that include the first day or exclude the last, adjust the interpretation according to the applicable rule.
