Discount Calculator

Find the discount amount and final price, with optional sales tax.

Price breakdown

How it works

Enter the original price, discount percentage, and an optional tax percentage. The discount is subtracted first; the tax is then applied to the discounted price.

Practical example

A R$ 100.00 item with a 20% discount costs R$ 80.00 before tax. With 5% tax on the discounted price, the final total is R$ 84.00.
