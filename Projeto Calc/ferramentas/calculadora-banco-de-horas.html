Updated balance

How it works

The calculator adds credited hours to the previous balance and subtracts debited hours. Enter decimal hours: for example, 1.5 means one hour and thirty minutes.

Practical example

A previous balance of 2.5 hours, 1.25 hours credited, and 0.5 hours debited results in a balance of 3.25 hours, or 3 hours and 15 minutes. Follow your workplace agreement for legal limits and settlement periods.
