Work Hours Calculator

Calculate your worked hours, subtract breaks, and compare the total with your expected shift to track your time bank.

Enter zero if there is no break to subtract.

Calculation result

How it works

The calculator converts the entered times into minutes, subtracts the break from the time between clock-in and clock-out, and compares the total worked time with the expected shift. The balance can be positive, negative, or zero. If the clock-out time is earlier than the clock-in time, the calculator treats the shift as ending the next day.

Practical example

A shift from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM with a 60-minute break results in 8 hours worked. Compared with an expected 8-hour shift, the daily balance is zero. This tool is informational; confirm working-time and time-bank rules with the procedures that apply to your workplace.
