Vacation Pay Calculator

Estimate gross vacation remuneration with the one-third addition.

This estimate includes the constitutional one-third addition. It does not calculate tax deductions or the sale of vacation days.

Gross estimate

How it works

The salary is prorated by the number of vacation days using a 30-day month. The calculator then adds one-third of that amount, before payroll deductions.

Practical example

For a R$ 3,000 monthly salary and 30 vacation days, the gross base is R$ 3,000 and the one-third addition is R$ 1,000, for an estimated total of R$ 4,000 before deductions.
