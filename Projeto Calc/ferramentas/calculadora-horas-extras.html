Estimate

How it works

The hourly rate is estimated by dividing the monthly salary by the monthly-hours divisor. Overtime pay is the hourly rate multiplied by overtime hours and by one plus the additional rate.

Practical example

For a R$ 3,000 salary, a 220-hour divisor, two overtime hours, and a 50% additional rate, the base hourly rate is R$ 13.64, the overtime rate is R$ 20.45 per hour, and the estimated gross total is R$ 40.91. This estimate does not include payroll deductions or collective-agreement details.
