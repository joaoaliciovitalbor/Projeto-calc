Cookie and browser-storage notice

This notice describes the storage and third-party requests used by the current prototype.

Current status

Office Utilities currently has no advertising or analytics scripts and does not set advertising cookies. The language selector and this notice use browser local storage to remember your choices.

Third-party library requests

When you use a tool that needs an external library, your browser may download that library from jsDelivr, cdnjs, or SheetJS CDN. Those providers can receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address, browser details, requested resource, and request time. The library download does not include the contents of the file you selected.

Future advertising and consent

If advertising or analytics is added, this notice and the Privacy Policy must be updated to identify the actual services and technologies before they are enabled. Closing this notice is not consent to advertising, analytics, or personalized ads.

Google requires publishers serving personalized ads in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland to use a Google-certified consent management platform integrated with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework. Confirm current requirements and configure an approved platform before serving ads to those regions.
